Enterprise systems, technical consulting, and customer delivery

I spent fifteen years at SS&C, progressing from enterprise application support into infrastructure engineering and technical consulting. I took ownership of customer implementations, complex integrations, production incidents, and infrastructure improvements across roughly 60 enterprise environments. I worked directly with banks, pension funds, healthcare organizations, and public agencies, coordinating technical decisions with customer IT and security teams as well as SS&C Engineering, Product, and Support.

I led a two engineer modernization effort covering approximately 150 Linux systems across customer environments, SS&C's private cloud, AWS, and Azure. I planned migrations, resolved application and integration dependencies, coordinated production changes, and verified the results. My responsibility was not simply to upgrade an operating system. It was to deliver a controlled transition without compromising the systems customers relied on.

I handled identity, network, and security integrations involving Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, ADFS, SAML, TLS certificates, and IPsec connections. I also owned difficult technical escalations, traced issues across systems and teams, and translated security requirements into configurations customers could test and operate.

I led the move from Nagios monitoring to Instana, reducing false positive alerts by roughly 90% and investigation effort by about half. I also owned the VMware Site Recovery Manager disaster recovery lifecycle, including replication readiness, recovery exercises, remediation, production cutovers, and validation. Those projects required direct technical work, clear decisions across teams, and accountability for outcomes.

Why AI governance

I have spent my career turning security requirements and operational risks into working systems. AI introduces different failure modes, but the decisions still require technical understanding, clear ownership, tested controls, and evidence that people can evaluate.

I bring that approach to AI governance through SafeGuard, my AI security lab, and Vera. I evaluate what a system actually does, test its boundaries, document the results, and identify changes that teams can implement and verify.
