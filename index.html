Working principles

Governance has to
survive contact
with reality.

A policy is only useful if someone can translate it into a control, test whether that control works, and explain the remaining risk.

01

Start with the actual use case.

Understand the system, who uses it, the data it touches, and what can go wrong before assigning a risk rating.

02

Separate claims from evidence.

Map applicable requirements to observable controls. A vendor assurance or passing unit test is not proof of every security property.

03

Make the next decision clear.

Explain findings in business terms, identify owners and mitigations, and leave space for human judgment and residual risk.
