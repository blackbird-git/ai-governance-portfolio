SafeGuard

An evidence-based review method for understanding the risks of AI connected to business systems.

Designed

A scoped assessment covering ownership, identities, data boundaries, permitted actions, integration security, and monitoring.

Frameworks

Mapped relevant controls to NIST AI RMF 1.0, CSF 2.0, and the Generative AI Profile.

Evidence

Published illustrative assessments for fictional accounting, healthcare, and energy organizations.

How it works

  1. Scope the workflowIdentify users, connected systems, data, and business consequences.
  2. Assess controlsMap expected safeguards to documents, configurations, and authorized checks.
  3. Report riskDistinguish verified observations from assumptions and prioritize findings.

Status & limitations

Assessment method and illustrative reports published. Supporting review software remains under development. Examples are not client engagements or formal certifications.
