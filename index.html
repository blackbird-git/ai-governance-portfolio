About

Ilya Mirkin

At SS&C Technologies, I spent 15 years working on enterprise software infrastructure and served as a lead technical consultant for 60 customers supporting over 1,000 users, ranging from privately held investment firms to global asset managers and Fortune 50 financial institutions, as well as government agencies and healthcare organizations. I owned implementations, integrations, infrastructure modernization, disaster recovery, and the production problems that came with them.

I started Kingfisher Advisory to focus on AI governance and technology strategy. That led to SafeGuard, a NIST-informed methodology for assessing AI integrations and their security controls. Alongside it, I've been building local AI projects to test authorization, model reliability, and other problems that are easier to understand by working directly with the technology.

Outside of work, you can find me backcountry hunting and fishing in the mountains of Idaho, or camping with my wife, our two kids, and our dogs.

AI governance and risk assessment

SafeGuard

A practical method for evaluating the risks of AI connected to business systems.

Developed an assessment process covering the workflow, data access, identity permissions, authorized actions, monitoring, and recovery. Mapped the review criteria to relevant outcomes from NIST AI RMF 1.0 and CSF 2.0, with an emphasis on evidence rather than vendor assurances.

Published the methodology and illustrative assessments for fictional organizations. The supporting software for managing evidence and findings is still being developed.

AI application security

AI Security Engineering Lab

A local environment for testing what an AI application should and shouldn't be allowed to do.

Built a Python and FastAPI application using Ollama and synthetic financial records. Used it to test unauthorized data and tool requests, prompt injection scenarios, and whether the application enforces access restrictions independently of the model's responses.

Connected security events to Wazuh and documented the test cases, observed behavior, and limits of the results.

Offline AI development

Vera

A local conversational memory engine focused on source accuracy and privacy.

Developed a Python memory engine using SQLite/FTS5, Ollama, and local language models. Built retrieval with source attribution, correction handling, and controlled memory updates, then evaluated how well the system answered questions across conversations.

Testing exposed cases where fluent answers invented details or omitted relevant evidence. Those failures are documented alongside the evaluation approach.
