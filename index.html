03 / Background & perspective

Why AI governance.

Much of my career has been spent translating requirements into systems that actually work—and explaining the risks when they don't. AI adds new failure modes, but many of the practical questions are familiar: who is responsible, what can the system access, how is it tested, and what happens when something goes wrong?

That's why my independent projects focus on evidence, controls, and accountable decisions, rather than treating a framework or a passing test as proof that a system is safe. I'm interested in roles where I can help teams put those principles into practice.
